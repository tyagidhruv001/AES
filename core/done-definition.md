# Definition of Done

A change is complete only when:
- Acceptance criteria are met or explicitly waived.
- Relevant tests are added/updated.
- Relevant tests and checks are executed, with outcomes reported.
- Security and data-integrity implications are reviewed.
- The final diff is scoped and reviewed.
- Documentation/configuration is updated where needed.
- Known limitations and unverified areas are stated.
- No critical/high-severity unresolved finding remains without explicit acceptance.
